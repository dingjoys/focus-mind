## 1. Executive Summary

**LaunchMinds** is an **AI-native brand campaign planning and end-to-end deployment platform** for companies and projects that want to drive user growth, community building, and brand awareness through structured campaigns.

The platform's core differentiator: **AI Agents deeply learn a brand's assets** (brand voice, historical campaign data, target user personas, competitive intelligence), and on that basis autonomously generate targeted campaign strategies — helping brands go from idea to execution across the full pipeline, rather than simply offering a tool for people to fill out forms.

**Analogy**: If Zealy / Galxe are "campaign management SaaS," LaunchMinds is "a brand's dedicated AI campaign strategy team."

## 2. Market Pain Points & Opportunity

### 2.1 Core Pain Points for Brands

**Pain Point 1: Campaign planning relies heavily on manual labor, with inconsistent creative quality**

Most brand campaigns are planned ad hoc by internal marketing teams or external agencies. Planning cycles are long (typically 2–4 weeks), and the connection to brand assets is often superficial. The core problem: **a lack of data-driven planning capability** — there's no clear reasoning for "why this campaign will motivate this specific user segment."

**Pain Point 2: Existing platforms are tools, not partners**

Platforms like Zealy, TaskOn, and Galxe are fundamentally about "publishing tasks and distributing rewards." Brands still have to figure out for themselves: what campaign to run, what tasks to design, how much to reward, and how to reach users. The platform only handles distribution, not strategy. This is an especially heavy burden for small-to-mid brands and resource-constrained Web3 projects.

**Pain Point 3: Campaign performance is hard to predict or attribute**

After a campaign launches, brands typically only get coarse-grained participation numbers. Which task design drove the most high-quality users? Which reward mechanism attracted farmers? Existing platforms can't answer these questions, so the next campaign is still planned by guesswork.

**Pain Point 4: Multi-channel deployment is extremely costly**

A single campaign usually needs to run simultaneously across Twitter/X, Discord, Telegram, on-chain tasks, and in-product touchpoints. Brands must coordinate multiple tools and teams, resulting in huge labor costs and coordination friction.

**Pain Point 5: Web3 campaign platforms are unfriendly to non-Web3 brands**

Leading platforms today (Galxe, Layer3) are deeply tied to on-chain behavior, creating a high barrier to entry and cognitive overhead for traditional brands or early-stage Web3 projects without tokens or on-chain assets — and the user experience doesn't match mainstream brand tone.

## 3. Product Solution

### LaunchMinds' Core Philosophy

> **"Not giving brands a better tool to run campaigns — having AI figure out the campaign strategy for them."**

LaunchMinds builds a **Brand Intelligence Layer**: after deeply absorbing a brand's assets, AI Agents act as the brand's "digital marketing brain," continuously producing targeted campaign strategies and directly driving execution.

### Workflow Overview

```
Brand Asset Input
    │
    ▼
Brand Agent Learning Layer
```

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Brand Intelligence Onboarding

When onboarding, brands submit:
- Brand overview, core value proposition, target user personas
- Historical campaign data (CSV / API import)
- Competitor list (platform auto-crawls public information)
- Social accounts (Twitter/X, Discord, Telegram)
- Product documentation or whitepaper (PDF/URL)

The **Brand Agent** absorbs these assets to generate the brand's "intelligence profile card," which serves as the contextual foundation for all subsequent planning.

---

### 4.2 AI Campaign Planner

A brand inputs a coarse-grained goal (e.g., "Add 5,000 high-quality Discord members this month"), and the Agent outputs a complete campaign plan draft, including:

- **Campaign theme & narrative framework** (customized to brand voice)
- **Task design** (recommended mix of on-chain, social, and in-product tasks)
- **Reward mechanics** (token rewards, NFTs, whitelist spots, points, physical goods — based on brand budget and historical conversion rates)
- **Target user segmentation** (recommended audiences based on historical data)
- **Timeline planning** (optimal split of warm-up, active, and settlement phases)
- **Budget allocation recommendations**
- **Expected ROI estimate** (based on historical data from similar campaigns)

Brands can revise the draft via natural-language instructions, and the Agent updates the plan in real time.

---

### 4.3 One-Click Multi-Channel Deployment

Once a plan is confirmed, LaunchMinds drives deployment directly:

| Channel | Capability |
|------|------|
| On-chain tasks | Smart contract interaction verification, NFT minting, token distribution |
| Creator tasks | Verified by view count, distributed automatically |
| Creator tasks | AI scores content quality + leaderboard; brand does a secondary review and distributes rewards manually |
| Twitter/X | Follow/retweet/quote verification + leaderboards |
| Discord | Bot integration, role distribution, in-server tasks |
| Telegram | Group task verification, bot automation |
| In-product | API/SDK integration to verify product behavior (sign-up, trading, holdings, etc.) |
| Email | Notification outreach, integrated with existing CRM |

---

### 4.4 Participant Intelligence

During and after a campaign, the platform provides:

- **User quality scoring**: distinguishes real users from farmers (based on on-chain behavior, social account history, participation patterns)
- **Task funnel analysis**: completion rate, drop-off points, and time-spent distribution for each task
- **High-value user identification**: which participants later convert into real, retained users
- **Competitive campaign benchmarking**: comparison against public data from concurrent competitor campaigns
- **Next-campaign recommendation report**: auto-generated by AI based on this campaign's data

---

### 4.5 Campaign Marketplace

Anonymized templates (with brand identity stripped) from successful campaigns enter the platform's template library. Other brands can reuse them with one click, and the Brand Agent automatically adapts them to their own brand voice — creating a data flywheel effect for the platform.

---

### 4.6 Data Sync (Enterprise Tier)

Modeled on Hightouch's core capability — **syncing user data from data warehouses to downstream tools** — the LaunchMinds Enterprise tier supports:

- Pulling user segments from a brand's data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, dbt)
- Precisely targeting different campaigns based on those segments
- Writing campaign participation data back to the brand's data warehouse
- Bi-directional sync with CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot

This makes LaunchMinds part of a brand's marketing data infrastructure rather than an isolated campaign tool, significantly increasing customer stickiness.

### 5.2 Structural Advantages Over Web3 Platforms

**Limitations of Zealy / TaskOn**: Fundamentally a "task board" — usable only after a brand has already done its own campaign planning, with no planning support provided; UX revolves around points and leaderboards with little brand customization; no localized service for non-Web3 brands.

**Limitations of Galxe**: Heavily dependent on on-chain credentials (OATs), deeply tied to Web3-native users; campaign formats are highly homogeneous, resulting in low brand differentiation; on-chain operations are a high barrier for mainstream users.

**Limitations of Layer3**: Positioned around Web3 user education, well suited to protocol projects but with limited support for brand-marketing use cases; task design is heavily standardized with weak customization.

### 5.3 Three Lessons from Hightouch

1. **Professionalism over consumerization**: Hightouch doesn't chase the largest user base — it goes deep with data teams and becomes infrastructure. LaunchMinds should similarly position itself as "an essential tool for a brand's growth team," not "yet another task-publishing platform."

2. **Deep integration at the data layer**: Hightouch's moat is its bi-directional connection to data warehouses. LaunchMinds' enterprise tier should make bi-directional sync between "campaign data ↔ brand data warehouse" its own moat.

3. **AI as a capability, not a feature**: Hightouch embeds AI into its core data flow rather than bolting it on as an "AI button." LaunchMinds' AI should be how the platform operates, not a menu item.

## 8. Technical Architecture

### 8.1 Core Tech Stack

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           Brand Intelligence Layer       │
│  ┌─────────────┐  ┌──────────────────┐  │
│  │ Brand Agent  │  │ Campaign Planner │  │
│  │ (RAG + LLM) │  │     Agent        │  │
│  └─────────────┘  └──────────────────┘  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
           │                  │
┌──────────▼──────────────────▼──────────┐
│          Orchestration Layer            │
│     (Task Queue · Workflow Engine)      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
           │
┌──────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
│         Integration Layer               │
│  Twitter  Discord  Telegram  On-chain   │
│   API      Bot      Bot      (EVM)      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
           │
┌──────────▼──────────────────────────────┐
│         Data & Analytics Layer          │
│   ClickHouse · dbt · Warehouse Sync     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 8.2 AI Capability Stack

- **LLM**: Minds API (primary plan generation) + GPT-4o (fallback)
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Brand document embeddings, with vector retrieval ensuring plans stay consistent with brand assets
- **Fine-tuning**: Continuously fine-tuned on accumulated campaign performance data to improve planning quality
- **Agent framework**: In-house multi-agent orchestration supporting the full plan → review → deploy → monitor pipeline

### 8.3 Core Moats

1. **Campaign data accumulation**: Participation and conversion data from every campaign becomes the platform's proprietary training data, compounding over time
2. **Brand Agent personalization**: Each brand's Agent improves significantly with long-term use, producing meaningfully better plans than for a newly onboarded brand — creating switching costs
3. **Multi-channel integration network**: Deep integration with mainstream platforms is an engineering moat that's hard for late entrants to quickly replicate

## 9. Go-to-Market Strategy

### 9.1 Phase 1: 0–6 Months (MVP Validation)

**Goal**: 20 paying brands, 5 high-quality flagship case studies

**Strategy**:
- Focus on the Web3 vertical (lower education cost, higher openness to new tools)
- Run the Brand Agent process manually at first (human + AI hybrid) to validate demand quickly
- Directly reach Web3 project CMOs (Twitter DMs + Discord communities + industry events)
- Provide deep, hands-on service per campaign to refine the product and accumulate case data

**Key Milestones**:
- [ ] 3 publicly referenceable success stories (campaign participants >1,000, brand satisfaction >8/10)
- [ ] Complete the core AI planning + multi-channel deployment closed loop

---

### 9.2 Phase 2: 7–12 Months (Growth Acceleration)

**Goal**: 200 paying customers; enter the non-Web3 brand market

**Strategy**:
- Open self-serve access (Growth Plan) to reduce acquisition friction
- Launch the Campaign Marketplace to attract small-to-mid brands to reuse templates
- Build a partner network (Web3 accelerators, marketing agencies, VC portfolios)
- Content marketing: publish industry campaign planning reports and Web3 community growth playbooks

---

### 9.3 Phase 3: 13–24 Months (Enterprise Expansion)

**Goal**: Enterprise customers (Fortune 500 sub-brands, large Web3 protocols)

**Strategy**:
- Launch the enterprise data warehouse integration (modeled on Hightouch's B2B playbook)
- Build a dedicated sales team covering enterprise accounts
- Internationalize (Asia markets: Korea, Japan, Southeast Asia Web3 markets)
